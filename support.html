FAQ

How do I sync between iPhone and iPad? Sign in to the same Apple ID on both devices and turn on iCloud for Relationship Radar (Settings > your name > iCloud). Settings in the app shows the sync status.

I deleted someone by mistake. Can I get them back? Yes. Deleted people stay in Recently deleted for 30 days. Open Settings > Recently deleted and tap Restore.

How does the Face ID / PIN lock work? Turn it on in Settings. The app then asks for Face ID or your app PIN when it opens, and before deleting a person or all data.

How do I restore my $4.99 unlock? Go to Settings and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I delete all my data? Settings > Delete all data. This removes everything from this device and from iCloud and can't be undone.
