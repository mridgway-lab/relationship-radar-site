Privacy Policy — Relationship Radar

Effective: November 18, 2026 · Publisher: DTRT Enterprises, LLC

Short version: we collect nothing. The app has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

What the app stores and where

  • Everything you enter (people, ratings, notes, settings) is stored on your device.
  • If iCloud is enabled on your device, the app syncs that data through your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). Only your Apple ID can access it; the developer cannot see, read, or access it.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.

App PIN and Face ID

The app requires a 4–6 digit PIN that you choose. Only a salted SHA-256 hash of it is stored, in the Keychain of each device (device-only; never in iCloud, never sent anywhere). You can optionally use Face ID as a shortcut. Face ID is handled entirely by iOS (Apple's LocalAuthentication); the app never receives or stores biometric data.

Location and weather (Disguise mode only)

Disguise mode is off by default. If you turn it on, the app opens to a weather radar map and asks for your location while the app is in use only. Your location is used on your device to center the map and is never collected, stored, or sent to the developer.

  • To show current conditions, the app sends your coordinates to Apple's WeatherKit service, and to Apple's map and place-name (geocoding) services. Apple says WeatherKit uses location only to provide weather data and doesn't link it to your Apple ID; see Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.
  • The radar overlay images are downloaded from the U.S. NOAA/National Weather Service nowCOAST service. Those requests contain only the map area being viewed (map tile boundaries), not your exact location or any identifier, but like any web request they reveal your IP address to NOAA's servers.
  • You can turn off Disguise mode or deny location at any time (the map then works without centering on you).

Notifications

The app doesn't send notifications. If it ever does, they will contain only generic text (no names or details).

Purchases

The optional $4.99 one-time unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer receives no payment card or personal information.

Tracking, analytics, ads

None. No third-party SDKs, no advertising identifiers, no cross-app tracking, no data sales.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Delete all data in the app. Deleted people stay in Settings → Recently deleted for 30 days so you can restore them; after that (or if you choose Erase now) they are permanently removed from your devices and your private iCloud. You can also delete the app. To remove synced copies, also delete the app's data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Age

The app is intended for adults 18 and over.
